Tribute to Charles Babbage

About Charles Babbage

Charles Babbage was an English polymath, mathematician, philosopher, inventor, and mechanical engineer, widely regarded as a pioneer of computing and often credited with originating the concept of a digital programmable computer.

Achievements

  • Designing the Difference Engine and the Analytical Engine.
  • Was a founding member of the Analytical Society.
  • Successfully broke the Vigenère autokey cipher.
  • Invented the speedometer and the locomotive cowcatcher.
